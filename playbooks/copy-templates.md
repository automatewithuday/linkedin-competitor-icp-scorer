# Copy Templates: Three Outreach Openers

Use these with your `ranked_engagers.csv`. Each template maps to a different signal combination from the CSV columns. Fill in the brackets; don't add fluff.

---

## Template 1: Pain-Pivot

**Use when:** `intent = medium`, `icp_fit >= 4`, no strong comment excerpt. They engaged but didn't signal a specific pain. You need to name the pain for them.

**Format:** Name their situation. Pivot to the pain it creates. One line on what you do. Small ask.

---

**LinkedIn DM (short form):**

> "[Name] - your engagement with [topic/theme] tells me [situation they're likely in]. That usually means [downstream pain]. We solve that specifically - [one-line category description]. Worth a quick exchange if it's still live?"

**Example filled in:**

> "Alex - your engagement with [founder]'s post on pipeline efficiency tells me you're likely wrestling with volume vs. quality tradeoffs. That usually means your reps are spending time on accounts that were never in-market. We built a way to find the ones who already raised their hand. Worth a quick exchange if it's still live?"

---

## Template 2: Assumed-Knowledge

**Use when:** `intent = high`, `comment_excerpt` is non-trivial. They named a problem, asked a question, or showed real category knowledge. You can open mid-conversation.

**Format:** Reference the idea they engaged with (not the fact that they liked/commented). Name their situation as a given. What you do. Tiny next step.

---

**LinkedIn connection note (max 300 chars):**

> "Your point on [idea from comment] is exactly the problem - most teams hit that wall and [common workaround]. We built the alternative. Connecting if this is still live for you."

**LinkedIn DM (longer form):**

> "Caught your comment on [topic]. The [specific point they raised] is usually the symptom - the root issue is [underlying cause]. That's the exact problem [your product/category] was built for. I won't bury the pitch: [one sentence on what you do]. If that matches where you're stuck, happy to show you specifically how it works for [their title/function]."

---

## Template 3: Integration-Partner

**Use when:** The target is adjacent to your buyer - they're a vendor, agency, or integration partner to your ICP rather than a direct buyer. `icp_fit = 3`. They engage heavily with the content but their title suggests they serve your buyers rather than being one.

**Format:** Acknowledge the shared customer base. Propose a referral or partnership angle. No pitch on your product.

---

**LinkedIn DM:**

> "[Name] - looks like you're working with a lot of [ICP description] based on your engagement with [topic]. We're seeing strong pull from that same segment and have had a few clients ask about [adjacent service/category you don't cover]. Would it make sense to compare notes? Not a pitch - genuinely curious if there's a referral angle."

---

## Column-to-template mapping

| `intent` | `icp_fit` | `comment_excerpt` | Use template |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| high | 4-5 | non-empty | Assumed-Knowledge |
| medium | 4-5 | empty or generic | Pain-Pivot |
| low | 4-5 | any | Pain-Pivot (softer tone) |
| any | 3 | any | Integration-Partner |
| any | 1-2 | any | Skip - not your buyer |

---

## Rules that apply to all three

- No em-dashes. Use periods or parentheses.
- No "I hope this finds you well" or "reaching out because."
- Name their category, not your product name, in the first message.
- The ask is always small - "worth a quick note back?" not "let's set up a 30-minute call."
- Max 5 sentences in a cold opener. If you need more, you don't understand the pain well enough yet.
